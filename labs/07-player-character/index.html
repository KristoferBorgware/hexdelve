You are the wanderer — a second character on the same 17-bone rig, carrying nothing. Click a hexagon to walk there, click again to run. Click the smith or his anvil and you walk over to watch him work. Click the helmet lying in the yard and he fetches it and puts it on; click him again to take it off.

Two characters, one system. The wanderer's pose comes from the blend tree under your clicks; the blacksmith's comes from the same player running his own schedule of blows. Neither knows about the other.

The helmet is a prop, not a character: no bones, one group, modelled around the head bone's origin. Picking it up re-parents it onto that bone and nothing else changes — every clip carries it from then on.

The smith stands where lab 06 measured: the spot is derived from his own swing, so his hammer reaches the anvil face and the IK has something to correct rather than something to invent.
