Lab 06 — Click to move

Click a hexagon to walk there. Click again while he is going and he runs. Click the anvil and he walks to the nearest tile beside it, steps off the grid to get within reach, strikes once, and steps back onto his hexagon.

Neighbouring tile centres are 1.73 m apart and his reach is about 1 m, so standing on the grid he would swing at nothing. The stance he steps out to is measured from the swing itself, not guessed.

A tile is only reachable if every step of the way rises or falls by at most one terrace, so the tall mesa has no route up it and he will say so rather than walking through the cliff.
